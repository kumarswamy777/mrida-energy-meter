Hardware Guide & OLED Screen

Instructions for on-device button and display navigation

SSD1306 OLED Display Operations
  • Short Press (< 2s) [GPIO 32]: Cycles between Screen 1 (Live Measurements & Balance) and Screen 2 (Safety Limits & Anti-Theft Status).
  • Long Press (> 2s) [GPIO 32]: Toggles manual override on the power load relay directly from the physical hardware.
  • Auto-Disconnect Protection: Circuit cuts off instantly when balance reaches ₹0.00 or if line voltage/current exceeds configured safety thresholds.
  • Anti-Theft Detection: If PZEM detects current flow while the relay is in OFF state, a theft warning is displayed and logged.
